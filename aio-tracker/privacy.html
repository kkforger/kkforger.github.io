Privacy Policy for AIO Tracker

App: AIO Tracker (com.kkforger.apps.aiotracker)
Developer: KKForger Apps
Effective Date: November 10, 2026
Contact: kkforger@gmail.com
Privacy-First Commitment: AIO Tracker is built as an offline-first tracker. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not maintain proprietary cloud servers storing your personal watchlists, habits, notes, or media libraries.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how KKForger Apps ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information in connection with your use of the AIO Tracker mobile application ("the App"). Your privacy is fundamental to our software design.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Library & Watchlist Data: Your custom lists, movies, TV shows, anime, books, video games, watched episode counts, personal ratings, and notes are stored strictly on your device inside an on-device SQLite database. This data is never uploaded to our servers.
  • App Lock & Biometric Authentication: If you enable PIN Lock or Biometric Unlock, your security PIN hash and recovery token are stored in your device's hardware-backed Android Keystore. Biometric verification (fingerprint or face) is performed entirely by Android OS system APIs (BiometricPrompt); the App never accesses or stores your raw biometric data.
  • Optional API Keys: Any optional personal API keys you choose to enter (e.g., TMDB or RAWG) are stored locally on your device in private app storage and sent only to those respective services over secure HTTPS connections.

3. Optional Google Account & Drive Backup

The App provides an optional backup feature allowing users to back up their data directly to their personal Google Account:

  • User-Initiated Backup: The App connects to your chosen Google Account solely to upload or restore your own compressed JSON backup file (AIO-Tracker-backup.json).
  • Limited Access: The App cannot view, modify, or delete any other files, folders, emails, or personal data in your Google Account.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: AIO Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

For users on the free tier, the App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, coarse location data, and ad interaction diagnostics to deliver relevant ads, prevent ad fraud, and report ad performance.

  • Google Advertising ID: AdMob may use your device's Google Advertising ID (AD_ID). You can inspect, reset, or delete this identifier at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Ad-Free Option: You can permanently disable all advertisements and tracking by upgrading to AIO Tracker Pro.
  • For more information on how Google handles advertising data, review Google's Privacy & Terms.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

All in-app purchases and subscription payments for AIO Tracker Pro are processed securely through Google Play Billing. KKForger Apps does not collect, receive, or store your credit card, debit card, UPI, bank account, or payment credentials. We store only an encrypted receipt token in your device's Keystore to verify your Pro entitlement offline.

6. Third-Party Metadata Services

To provide posters, synopses, and release dates, the App communicates with public metadata APIs over HTTPS:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB): Used for film and TV metadata. (Not certified by TMDB).
  • TVmaze: Used for television broadcast schedules and episode names under CC BY-SA.
  • Open Library: Used for book descriptions and cover art via the Internet Archive.
  • RAWG Video Games Database: Used for video game metadata.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons: Used for public domain encyclopedic media summaries under CC BY-SA.

7. Children's Privacy

AIO Tracker is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention, Export & Deletion

Because your data resides on your physical device, you maintain full sovereignty over it at all times:

  • Export: You can export your entire library in open JSON or CSV formats at any time via Settings → Import & Export.
  • Complete Erasure: You can permanently delete all library items, cached images, backups, and Keystore keys immediately by selecting Settings → Security → Erase All Data, or by uninstalling the application from your device.
